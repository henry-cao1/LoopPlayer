The app embeds no font files; it references fonts already installed by the OS:

5. 示例音乐 / Demo audio
----------------------------------------------------------------
samples/light-loop-demo.wav（82 秒）由本项目的脚本 tools/gen_sample.py 用数学方式合成：
正弦谐波叠加 + 固定随机种子的噪声，不含任何采样素材、循环素材或第三方录音。
The 82-second demo track is synthesised by tools/gen_sample.py from sine partials and
seeded noise. It contains no samples, loops or third-party recordings.

  版权 / Copyright (c) 2026 LoopPlayer
  许可 / License: 与本项目一致（MIT）

详见 samples/LICENSE.txt。测试用音频（tools/.smoke-assets）不随发行包分发。
See samples/LICENSE.txt. Test audio under tools/.smoke-assets is not shipped.
